# Technical Seminar: Blue Brain Technology

Slides from my undergraduate technical seminar, presented in March 2023 for the BE in Computer Science and Engineering at Dayananda Sagar Academy of Technology and Management (DSATM), Bengaluru.

- **Presenter:** Madhu Sudhan S (1DT19CS074)
- **Guide:** Mr Sreenidhi B S, Associate Professor, Dept of CSE
- **Slides:** [Technical Seminar ppt - M.pptx](Technical%20Seminar%20ppt%20-%20M.pptx) (17 slides, PowerPoint)

## What the seminar covered

The talk introduced the **Blue Brain Project**, the EPFL-led effort (run on IBM supercomputers in Lausanne) to reverse-engineer the mammalian brain and rebuild it as a detailed computer simulation, a "virtual brain".

| # | Section | In short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction | Why the idea of a virtual brain is interesting: keeping knowledge that is otherwise lost |
| 2 | What is Blue Brain? | Reverse-engineering the brain to build a full computer model of it |
| 3 | How the natural brain works | Sensory input, integration and motor output |
| 4 | Simulation and uploading | How a brain might be scanned and uploaded, including the proposed use of nanobots |
| 5 | Blue Brain power and the project at Lausanne | What the model could reveal about memory and brain circuits, and its use for diseases such as Parkinson's |
| 6 | Advantages and disadvantages | Preserving intelligence versus dependence on computers, security threats and misuse |
| 7 | Simulation hardware | One processor per neuron for the 10,000-neuron cortical column of a rat |
| 8 | Benefits and applications | Neural code, neocortical processing, drug discovery, whole-brain and molecular modelling |
| 9 | Challenges | Neural complexity, scale, interconnectivity |
| 10 | Conclusion and references | Outlook, plus sources including Markram (2006, *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*) and his 2009 TED talk |
